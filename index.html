Sanrakshan 2025 · Noida

PGP Sustainable Management

The Post Graduate Program in Sustainable Management (PGPSM) is a two-year, full-time residential programme run by IIM Lucknow at its Noida campus. It develops managers with an integrated understanding of systems thinking, stakeholder management, lifecycle analysis, public externalities and social entrepreneurship.

Centre of Business Sustainability

The Centre of Business Sustainability (CBS) brings together industry specialists and academic experts. It shares corporate best practice through research, executive seminars, webinars and sustainability roundtables.

02The 2026 vision

What's new this year

Our focus for 2026: building resilient businesses through sustainability and AI.

i. AI

AI for Emissions Tracking

Hackathon-style challenges where teams use machine learning to track industrial emissions and supply-chain data in real time.

ii. Startups

A Bigger Startup Exhibition

More space for startups to exhibit and pitch live to clean-energy investors and venture networks.

iii. Talent

A Talent Showcase for Recruiters

Finalists' profiles shared with partner companies looking for sustainability-focused managers.
