CBS — POST GRADUATE PROGRAM IN SUSTAINABLE MANAGEMENT

PGP Sustainable Management

The Post Graduate Program in Sustainable Management (PGPSM) is a top-tier two-year full-time residential course conducted by IIM Lucknow at its Noida campus. The curriculum produces quality corporate managers with an integrated understanding of systems thinking, stakeholder management, lifecycle tracking, public externalities, and social entrepreneurship parameters.

The CBS Excellence Hub

The Centre of Business Sustainability (CBS) serves as a collaborative multidisciplinary body of industry specialists and experts. CBS focuses on actionable dissemination and bringing together corporate best practices via specialized research, executive seminars, webinars, and active sustainability roundtables.
